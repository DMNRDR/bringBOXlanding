Questions

Do I have to leave Wolt?
No. Most businesses keep the platform for new customers and send their regulars to their own site.
I don't have couriers.
Start with pickup only, or with one part-time driver. You set the delivery zones and fees yourself.
Who installs it?
We do. On your server, with your domain. Then we train you and leave.
What if something breaks?
The system backs itself up every night. The first year of updates is included; each further year is €390.
How do customers pay?
By card through your own Stripe account, or in cash on delivery. The money goes straight to you.
What if you stop BringBox?
Your server keeps running the last version. The source code is held in escrow for you.
